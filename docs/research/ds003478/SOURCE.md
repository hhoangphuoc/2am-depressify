# OpenNeuro ds003478: "EEG: Depression rest"

- **Dataset:** https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds003478/versions/1.1.0 · DOI `10.18112/openneuro.ds003478.v1.1.0` · license **CC0**
- **Paper:** Cavanagh JF, Bismark AW, Frank MJ, Allen JJB. *Multiple Dissociations Between Comorbid Depression and Anxiety on Reward and Punishment Processing: Evidence From Computationally Informed EEG.* Computational Psychiatry 2019;3:1–17. doi:10.1162/cpsy_a_00024 (PMID 31149639)
- **Vendored here:** only `data/participants.tsv` (122 rows). The EEG files are not downloaded.

## What it is
- Resting-state EEG: two 6-minute runs with alternating 1-minute eyes-open/closed blocks, at **one lab visit**.
- 122 college students (18–24) from the J.J.B. Allen lab, University of Arizona, recorded circa 2008–2010. They were selected for reliably **high or low BDI**.
- Columns: `sex` (coding undocumented), `age`, `BDI`, `STAI`, `SCID` (group), `SCID_notes`, `HamD`. SCID and HamD exist only for the 32 interviewed participants, all from the high-BDI group.

## What it is not
- No TMS, treatment, follow-up, or phone/wearable data.
- Not a post-TMS or severe-depression population.
- README caveats: HEOG/VEOG may be mislabelled, some channels are interpolated, sub-516 has no second rest run, and Original_ID 544 was excluded by the authors (unstable BDI).

## How this project uses it
Seed realism for synthetic demo patients plus one pitch claim. It is **not** used in the runtime loop and no thresholds come from it. See `findings.md` (generated by `stats.mjs`) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-open-data-ds003478-design.md`.
