Part 1

Patient app

iPhone 393 × 852. One main action per screen. Patients never see Attention flags or scores.

First use

One plain promise, then a real choice about sharing. Declining never blocks the app.

Today

One invitation. The greeting follows the time of day; after 22:00 the app turns dark and softer.

Voice Check-in · Gemini Live

A real-time voice session. The orb is the only focal point; its colour shows who is talking and its size follows the audio. The live caption is always on.

Voice Check-in · one idea

Tool calls run quietly, then one Suggestion arrives as a card the Patient can accept by voice or touch.

Voice Check-in · interruptions

Every break in the session keeps what was said and offers one calm way on.

Typed, booking and pauses

The same conversation by text, an explicit agreement before anything reaches the calendar, and a calm pause.

Finishing and Summary

Three optional ratings, then the same Summary the Clinician reads.

History

A calm record. Missing days are gaps, never zero.

Settings and Consent

Each Signal has its own Consent, and stopping deletes what was collected.

Privacy, quiet hours and support

Sharing can be withdrawn at any time. Help is human and the app never dials.

Crisis

Appears before any network call. The Patient places the call; the app never does.

Voice session states

Each state maps to an event in the Gemini Live API. The orb changes colour on every state, including under Reduce Motion, where its size stays still.
